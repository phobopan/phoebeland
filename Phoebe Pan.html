§ 01 — About
In the round
Reading time · 40s
BasedSan Francisco / NYC / HK
Pronounsshe/her
Years at itlong enough
Open tocollaborations, letters

I build small tools, research the quiet mechanics of new technology, invest in companies run by people I admire, and take studio classes in contemporary and classical dance. I believe in legible interfaces, long walks, and the sentence as a unit of thought.

Current obsessions — post-rationalist design
— vector databases, quietly
— wooden spoons
— Anna Halprin’s scores
— the subjunctive mood

Not doing — performative busyness
— meetings that could be letters
